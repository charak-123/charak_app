# Charak — Telemedicine Consent & Disclaimer

**Draft version: 2026-10-01. For legal review.** Aligned to the Telemedicine
Practice Guidelines 2020 (India). Patients accept this before their first
consultation.

## Please read before consulting
- A **teleconsultation** is a remote consultation (chat/voice/video and shared
  symptom information). It is **not a substitute for in-person examination** and
  may have limitations; the doctor may advise an in-person visit or tests.
- The **doctor decides** whether a condition is suitable for teleconsultation and
  may decline or refer you.
- **In an emergency, do not use Charak** — contact your local emergency services
  or go to the nearest hospital.
- A prescription, if issued, is at the doctor's professional discretion and
  follows applicable rules; some medicines cannot be prescribed remotely.

## What you consent to
- Consulting a registered doctor through Charak remotely.
- Sharing the symptom information you provide (including photos, video and voice
  notes) with that doctor, and Charak processing it — including converting voice
  notes to text — to deliver and record the consultation.
- Charak and the doctor retaining consultation records as required by law.

## Your acknowledgements
- The information you give is accurate and complete to the best of your
  knowledge.
- You understand the limitations of remote care described above.
- You may withdraw consent and discontinue at any time; this will not affect care
  already provided.
