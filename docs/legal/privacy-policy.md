# Charak — Privacy Policy

**Draft version: 2026-10-01. For legal review.**

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Charak", "we") operates the Charak patient and doctor
apps, which connect patients with doctors for online consultations and home
visits. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights
under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

## 1. Data we collect
- **Account & identity:** phone number, name, role (patient/doctor).
- **Patients:** saved addresses and precise location (only when you add a
  home-visit address, to check it is inside a doctor's service area); symptom
  information you provide — photos, video, voice notes and their transcripts
  ("intake media"); booking, payment and complaint history.
- **Doctors:** professional details, licence/registration number and
  verification documents, service area and base location, pricing, bank/payout
  details, ratings.
- **Technical:** device push token (for notifications), app/diagnostic logs,
  IP address.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell
  personal data.

## 2. Why we use it (purposes)
To create and operate your account; match patients and doctors; run
consultations and home visits; process payments and doctor payouts; send
service notifications; transcribe voice notes into text for the doctor; handle
complaints; meet legal, tax and medical-record obligations; and keep the service
secure.

## 3. Health data
Symptom information is sensitive personal data. It is shared only with the doctor
for your booking and the staff who operate the service, is processed on servers
located in India where feasible, and is never used for advertising.

## 4. Sharing
We share data only with: the doctor/patient on your booking; processors acting
on our instructions — payments ([Razorpay]), SMS/OTP ([MSG91]), calls ([Agora]),
cloud hosting and voice transcription ([Google Cloud / Vertex AI], regional
endpoint); and authorities where the law requires.

## 5. Retention
We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Booking,
payment, payout and consultation records are retained for [RETENTION PERIOD,
e.g. the period required by tax law and the applicable medical-record rules]
even after you delete your account, in de-identified form where possible.

## 6. Your rights (DPDP)
You may access, correct, or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and
nominate another person to exercise your rights. **Deleting your account**
(Profile → Delete account) erases your personal data after a 30-day grace
period, keeping only the de-identified records we are legally required to retain.
To exercise any right or raise a grievance, contact our Data Protection /
Grievance Officer: **[NAME, EMAIL, ADDRESS]**. If unsatisfied you may complain to
the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Security
Credentials are stored in the device keystore/keychain; data in transit is
encrypted (HTTPS); access is restricted on a need-to-know basis.

## 8. Children
The app is not intended for users under 18 without a parent/guardian acting on
their behalf.

## 9. Changes
We will notify you in-app of material changes and ask you to accept the updated
policy. Contact: **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.
